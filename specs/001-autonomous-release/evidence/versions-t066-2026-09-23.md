# T066 slice — the owner's operating versions over the durable US6 chain (2026-09-23)

Status: **slice landed; T066 stays open.** The paired-execution round and artifact-comparison views

## What landed

- **Durable US6 chain** (`app/services/growth_store.py`): comparison plan, recorded rounds and
  frozen candidate bundles are each persisted once, at an identity derived from their own
  content-derived ref, together with their exact issue input.
  - Resume re-issues each value through the same framework function and refuses anything that
    does not read back byte-for-byte.
  - `restore_validation_report` (validation.py) rebuilds a report only over its stored candidate
    and the stored rounds its gates cite. Every gate is re-parsed under the issue-time rules, and
    the status has to follow again. No dataset is re-exposed.
- **Versions** (`app/services/versions.py`, the `versions-v1` contribution: five routes, installed
  count 54 → 59).
  - The owner adopts the operating environment once.
  - Candidates are the persisted validation reports whose chain resumes. Their gates are shown
    with reasons, and `approvable` requires a passed sealed-offline report.
  - Approve, reject and defer go through `PersistentPromotionApprovals`, bound to the bundle, the
    report and the current environment.
  - Apply goes through `record_promotion_decision` and `activate_candidate` on the exact revision
    shown, and an approval is consumed once.
  - Rollback requires a stated reason. It restores the last retired version, keeps the rolled-back
    bundle out without a fresh approval, and records `external_effects_reverted: false`.
  - Experiments are the newest revision of each growth loop, resumed exactly, with the stop reason
    the loop recorded.
- **GUI** (`app/static/versions.mjs`, `versions.html`, `versions-page.mjs`, linked from the records
  page):
  - Shows the current version and its history, and each candidate's gates with their reasons.
  - Approve is offered only when the candidate is approvable. Applying is a separate button that
    appears after an approval.
  - Rollback asks for a reason and says that it does not undo what already went outside.
  - Each experiment shows its stop-reason explanation and best round.
